εEllada

Pay creators. Talk to the creator.

Ellada is a creator platform where every message carries a label showing who wrote it. Fans pay for clear, defined interactions, and creators keep more of what they earn.

The problem

Fans pay for closeness they can't verify

Hidden ghostwritersOn big subscription platforms, many top accounts use paid agency "chatters". Fans believe they're talking to the creator and usually aren't told otherwise.
Scam accountsOn Telegram and similar apps, accounts with stolen photos and chatbots take gifts and Stars, then deliver nothing.
Same script foreverThe relationship never changes. Every fan gets the same upsell loop, so trust and retention drop.
High platform cutThe market leader takes 20% of creator earnings, before agency fees.
The solution

Three rules built into the product

Written by creator

Labelled messages

Messages sent from the creator's verified device get a green seal. Team or AI replies are allowed but always labelled.

Fixed price

Defined offers

Fans buy concrete things: a voice note, a 10-minute call, a reply within 48 hours. Missed deadlines refund automatically.

Level 3

Relationships that grow

Fan levels unlock new kinds of contact over time, from messages to voice to live calls, so the interaction changes.

Retention

Why fans stay after levelling up

We don't sell content that can be copied. We sell a relationship that can't be.

Levels unlock accessHigher levels open the right to book calls and get personal replies. A screenshot captures nothing a fan can take with them.
Each use is paidUnlocked offers still cost money when used. A fan who leaves has already paid for everything they got.
Levels fadeAfter 30 days without any interaction, a fan drops one level. Coming back means rebuilding, so ghosting has a cost.
Leaks are traceableEvery media file carries an invisible watermark tied to the buyer. Leakers lose their account and levels, and we file takedowns.
Personal is worth less to othersA voice note that says the fan's name and answers their question has little value to anyone else.
